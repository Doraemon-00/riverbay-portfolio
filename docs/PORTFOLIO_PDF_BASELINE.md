# Portfolio PDF baseline: structure recommendation

Recorded 20 October 2026.

**Status: proposed baseline, not a final template or implementation approval.** Saved for future PDF content/layout decisions. Page count, artwork and typography remain adjustable after reviewing a complete sample domain spread.

## Purpose

A photo-led learning story with enough structure to compare progress and enough room for each child's individuality. Populate a prepared layout from structured, teacher-approved content. AI assists narrative drafting; it does not decide layout, infer ratings or analyse photos.

For the prototype, approximately **10 pages and 10 photos** should demonstrate the workflow. This is a proposed prototype page count, not a school requirement to replace the existing approximately 20-page booklet. Carol confirmed 10 demo photos, approximately 20 photos for the eventual product and an average final PDF size around 50 MB. The prototype need not reproduce that file size.

## Suggested page structure

| Pages | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover: child's name, class, semester/year and one main photo. Fictional Riverbay branding; light animal/floral decoration. |
| 2 | Learning at a glance: four domains and clearly labelled current indicators, with a brief explanation of the scale. Semester 2 also shows Semester 1 ratings. Missing prior ratings say “Not recorded”. |
| 3–4 | English: teacher-approved narrative, current indicator, selected photos and optional captions. |
| 5–6 | Chinese 中文: the same structure, with narrative and captions in Simplified Chinese. |
| 7–8 | Mathematics: the same structure. |
| 9–10 | Social-emotional development: the same structure. |

English is the main report language; Chinese-domain content is Chinese. Repeating child name, class and semester on every page is unnecessary. Keep any later navigation/footer treatment minimal.

## Photo allocation

- One cover photo.
- Two photos per domain: eight total.
- One additional photo in whichever domain benefits most.
- Ten photos is the demonstration assumption, not a mandatory completion count. Do not force teachers to fill slots or leave an awkward empty frame when fewer photos are supplied.
- Use fictional/placeholder imagery only. Teachers explicitly allocate images to sections; no automated inference about the child from photos.

## Each domain contains

1. **Learning indicator:** a labelled segmented ring; include a Semester 1 comparison where available and applicable. It is a broad indicator, not a complete account of the child's strengths. A missing current assessment must not appear as the lowest level.
2. **Learning story:** one teacher-approved paragraph selecting and connecting meaningful observations. Preserve context and assistance needed. Use dates/months when useful; avoid a forced chronological list or unsupported educational conclusions.
3. **Moments of learning:** photos with short, optional teacher-written captions explaining the activity or directly observed learning.

Example caption, only when supported by the teacher's observation:

> Arranging shells in a line helped with keeping track while counting.

Narrative length should be checked against a sample layout, not padded to fill space. Current experimental soft targets are approximately 80–120 English words or 140–220 Chinese characters for rich evidence; sparse evidence can yield shorter text. These are not final PDF constraints. Chinese fonts must be embedded and overflow must be handled without cutting off text.

## Content boundaries

- Raw observation timelines, internal notes, translation previews and workflow statuses stay in the teacher workspace.
- Avoid repetitive introductions, repeated child details and an additional AI-written overview that duplicates domain narratives.
- No compulsory “next steps” section initially. Carol has not requested it; any future goals should be explicitly supplied/approved by teachers rather than invented by the model.
- Cross-semester AI narrative synthesis is deferred. Drafting evaluation uses selected-semester evidence only. Showing Semester 1 ratings in Semester 2 is a separate required comparison and does not imply that previous narratives are sent to AI.
- No Peterhouse branding. Riverbay is the fictional demonstration identity.

## Suggested next design step

Review this content structure, then create **one complete domain spread** before laying out the whole booklet. Check narrative length, photo sizes/crops, caption usefulness, whitespace, Chinese typography and longer-content overflow. Decide the final cover/report artwork and page count after that review.
